# Zandy Touch staff help
## getting-started | Getting started | roles: owner, support | pages: A1 | group: Getting started
What the staff area is for, how to sign in, and what each menu item does.
The staff area is where the Zandy Touch team looks after the online shop. Customers place their own orders in the shop. You use the staff area to deal with those orders, keep the products and stock correct, and read messages from customers.

### What you can do here
- **Orders**: see new orders, agree the delivery fee with the customer, confirm, send out and close each order.
- **Products**: add and change products, their prices and their pictures, and bring back products you removed.
- **Import**: add or update many products at once from a spreadsheet.
- **Inbox**: read messages from the contact form and see the newsletter list.
- **My account**: change your own password.
- **Help**: this help. Most pages also have a small "How do I...?" link beside the page title that opens the right topic.

You cannot create an order for a customer. If someone phones or sends a WhatsApp message to order, send them the link to the product and ask them to order in the shop.

### Signing in
1. Open the staff sign-in page. It is called **Staff sign in**.
2. Type your **Email** and your **Password**.
3. Press **Sign in**.
4. You land on **Orders**, the home page of the staff area.

If you see "Email or password is incorrect.", check both and try again. After five wrong tries you must wait a few minutes. The message is "Too many attempts. Please wait a few minutes and try again."

### Your first sign-in with a temporary password
New support staff get a temporary password from the owner. It only works to get you started.
1. Sign in with your email and the temporary password.
2. The **My account** page opens by itself. It says "You are using a temporary password. Choose your own password to continue." The menu is hidden until you finish.
3. Under **Change password**, type the temporary password in **Current password**.
4. Type your own new password in **New password**. It must have at least 10 characters.
5. Press **Change password**. You see "Your password has been changed."
6. The menu now appears. Open **Orders** to start work.

Choose a password that you do not use anywhere else, and do not share it. See [Changing your password](help:password) for more.

### Finding your way around
The menu is at the top of every page. The name and role of the person signed in are shown at the top right, next to **Sign out**. The page you are on is marked in the menu.

The **Orders** and **Products** pages have a search box. Use it before you scroll.

> Good to know: times in the staff area are shown in Kampala time, for example "25 Oct 2026, 17:40 (Kampala time)". You do not need to add or subtract anything.
> Good to know: if you are signed out after a long time, just sign in again. You do not lose saved work.
> Good to know: always press **Sign out** when you finish on a shared phone or computer.
> Good to know: if a page says "This record was updated by someone else a moment ago", a colleague changed the same thing. The latest version has been loaded. Check it, then do your change again.

## orders | Handling orders | roles: owner, support | pages: A4, A5 | group: Orders
Find a new order, agree and record the delivery fee, confirm it, send it out, close it, or cancel it.
Every order comes from a customer in the shop. The customer pays cash or mobile money when the goods arrive. The delivery fee is not worked out by the website. You agree it with the customer, then type it in.

### Finding new orders
When an order is placed, the owner and staff who have alerts switched on get an email called "New order" with a link to the order. You can also open [Orders](page:A4) at any time.
1. Open **Orders** in the menu.
2. Look at the tabs: **All**, **New**, **Confirmed**, **Out for delivery**, **Delivered**, **Cancelled**. The number in brackets is how many orders are in each tab.
3. Press **New** to see orders nobody has dealt with yet. New orders are highlighted.
4. To find one order, type its order number, the customer's name or the phone number into **Search by order number, name or phone** and press **Search**.
5. Press the order number in the **Order** column to open the order.

In the list, the **Fee** column says **Not set** in bold when an open order has no delivery fee recorded yet. The **Placed** column shows the date and time in Kampala time.

### The order page
The top shows the order number, its status and when it was placed. You then see:
- **Customer and delivery**: name, phone (press it to call), a **WhatsApp** link, address, directions, how the customer likes to be contacted, and any customer note.
- **Items**: each product, the price at the time of the order, the quantity and the line total, then the **Subtotal**.
- **Delivery fee and total**: where you record the fee.
- **Actions**: the buttons for the next step.
- **Agreement record**: shows which version of the order terms the customer accepted, when, and who.
- **History**: every status change, who did it and when.

### Agreeing and recording the delivery fee
1. Open the order and call or WhatsApp the customer. Agree the delivery fee and the delivery time.
2. In **Delivery fee and total**, type the fee in **Agreed delivery fee (UGX)**. Use whole numbers only, for example 10000. Type 0 if delivery is free. The most you can type is 1,000,000.
3. If you want, write something in **Internal note**. Only staff see it.
4. Look at the **Total** line. It shows the items plus the fee.
5. Press **Save**. You see "Saved."

Until you press **Save**, the fee is not recorded, and the buttons below stay switched off. The page says "Record the delivery fee first."

> Good to know: the customer can see the fee and the total on the track-your-order page as soon as you save it.
> Good to know: you can still correct the fee while the order is New, Confirmed or Out for delivery. Once the order is Delivered or Cancelled, it is closed and cannot be changed.

### Confirming the order
1. Record the fee first (see above).
2. Press **Confirm order** in **Actions**.
3. A box called **Confirm order** opens and shows the order total.
4. If you want, add a **Note**.
5. Press **Confirm**. You see "Order marked: Confirmed" and the status changes.

### Approving delivery
Do this when the goods are leaving the shop.
1. Open a Confirmed order.
2. Press **Approve delivery**.
3. Check the total in the box, add a **Note** if you like (for example the name of the rider), then press **Confirm**.
4. The status becomes **Out for delivery**. The customer sees "On its way".

You cannot approve delivery without a recorded fee.

### Marking an order delivered
1. When the customer has received the goods and paid, open the order.
2. Press **Mark delivered**.
3. Under **How did the customer pay?**, choose **Cash** or **Mobile money**.
4. Press **Confirm**. The order is now closed.

### Cancelling an order
You can cancel while the order is New, Confirmed or Out for delivery. Use it when the customer does not agree the fee, wants to change what they ordered, or refuses the goods.
1. Open the order and press **Cancel order**.
2. A box opens. Read the yellow or blue message about stock (see below).
3. Type a reason in **Reason (not shown to the customer)**, at least 3 letters. For example "customer did not agree the fee".
4. Press **Cancel order** in the box. Press **Back** if you changed your mind.
5. You see a message at the top of the page about the stock.

What happens to stock:
- **Cancelled before dispatch** (New or Confirmed): the box says "Stock for these items will be returned." The items go back into stock straight away. The message afterwards is "Order cancelled. Stock for these items has been returned."
- **Cancelled after dispatch** (Out for delivery): the box says "Stock will not be returned automatically. Adjust stock if the goods come back." Nothing is added to stock. The message afterwards is "Order cancelled. Stock was not returned automatically; adjust stock if the goods come back." When the goods are back in the shop, add them yourself. See [Stock](help:stock).

> Good to know: orders cannot be edited. If a customer wants different items or quantities, cancel the order and ask them to order again in the shop.
> Good to know: a cancelled or delivered order cannot be opened again. This cannot be undone.
> Good to know: the price on an order is the price on the day it was placed. Changing a product's price later does not change old orders.
> Good to know: customers are not emailed when you change an order's status. They can see it on the track-your-order page, and you should tell them yourself by phone or WhatsApp.
> Good to know: customer contact details are removed automatically about 6 months after the order is closed.
> Good to know: after a cancellation, the product can take about a minute and a half to show as available again in the shop.

## products | Adding and changing products | roles: owner, support | pages: A7, A8 | group: Products and stock
Add a product by hand, change it, manage its pictures, publish it, remove it and bring it back.
A product can be a **draft**, **published** or **archived** (removed from the shop). Customers only see published products. A draft is invisible to customers, so you can take your time.

### Adding a product by hand
1. Open **Products** in the menu.
2. Press **New product**. The **New product** page opens.
3. Under **Basics**, fill in **Name**, **Brand** and **Category**. Choose the category by its name from the list of six, for example Body care. Dietary supplements and the weight-gain porridge belong in Health & Wellness.
4. Tick **Zandy Touch own brand** if it is your own product.
5. Fill in **SKU**. The page says it is optional, but fill it in anyway. A SKU is a short code for the product, such as ZT-SKIN-001, using letters, numbers and hyphens. Every product needs its own SKU. You need one to import products or pictures in bulk later.
6. Under **Price**, type the price in **Price (UGX)**, as a whole number such as 85000. You can leave it empty while the product is a draft.
7. Under **Stock**, type how many you have in **Opening stock**. Leave 0 if you have none yet.
8. Under **Descriptions**, fill in what you know: **Short description** (up to 200 characters), **Description**, **Size**, **How to use** and **Ingredients**. Write plain facts. Do not promise results.
9. Leave **Status** on "Draft (not visible to customers)" for now.
10. Press **Save**. You see "Product saved." and the product's own page opens, where you can add pictures.

### Pictures on a product
Pictures can be added only after the product has been saved once. On the **New product** page the Pictures section says "Save the product first, then add its pictures here."
1. On the product's page, find the **Pictures** section.
2. Under **Add pictures** (or **Add more pictures** if there are some already), choose one or more files. They must be JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB each. You can also drop files on the box.
3. The pictures are added to the product at once. A list shows each one.
4. The first picture is the **Main picture**. It is the one customers see first. Press **Earlier** or **Later** to move a picture.
5. Type a few words in **Alt text** to describe each picture, for example "Gold bottle of Signature perfume". This helps people who cannot see the picture.
6. To take a picture away, press **Remove**. A yellow message says how many pictures will be deleted when you save.
7. Press **Save pictures** (or the main **Save** button) to keep the changes to order, alt text and removed pictures. The message "Pictures saved." appears.

> Good to know: a product can have at most 6 pictures. When it has 6, the page says "This product has 6 pictures, the most it can have. Remove one to add another."
> Good to know: a picture that you remove and save is deleted for good.
> Good to know: new pictures are added at once, but order, alt text and removals only take effect when you press **Save pictures** or **Save**.

### Publishing rules
A product can be published only when it has all three of these:
- a price,
- at least one picture,
- a category.

1. Open the product (see "Changing a product" below).
2. Under **Publishing**, choose **Published**.
3. Press **Save**.
4. If something is missing, a red message tells you what. For example "A product needs a price before it can be published." Fix it and save again.
5. When it works, a **View on shop** button appears. Press it to see the product as a customer does.

A published product with no stock shows as Sold out in the shop. It is not hidden.

Tick **Featured on the home page** if you want the product shown on the home page.

> Good to know: a new product cannot be published at the first save because it has no picture yet. Save it as a draft, add the pictures, then publish.
> Good to know: the shop can take up to about a minute and a half to show a change. The staff screens always show the latest.
> Good to know: if you change a price, only new orders use it. Orders already placed keep the old price.

### Changing a product
1. Open **Products**. Type the name or SKU into **Search by name or SKU** and press **Search** if needed.
2. Press the product's name, or press **Edit**.
3. The page shows when (in Kampala time) and by whom it was last updated. Change what you need.
4. Press **Save**.

The stock number cannot be changed on this form. Use **Adjust stock**. See [Stock](help:stock).

### Removing a product
Removing hides the product from the shop. It is called archiving. The product is not lost, and you can bring it back later.
1. In **Products**, press **Remove** next to the product, or open the product and press **Remove from shop** at the bottom.
2. A box asks "Remove (product name)?" It says: "This archives the product. It leaves the shop, search and sitemap. Orders and stock history are kept. You can bring it back later from the Archived tab with Restore." Press **Archive product**, or press **Cancel** to leave the product as it is.
3. You see "Product removed from the shop (archived)."

The product leaves the shop, search and the sitemap. Old orders and the stock history are kept, even for orders that are still open.

### Bringing a removed product back
Restoring publishes the product again, so the publishing rules apply: it needs a price, a picture and a category.
1. In **Products**, press the **Archived** tab.
2. Find the product. Archived products have only two buttons: **Edit** and **Restore**. There is no **Adjust stock** or **Remove** there.
3. Press **Restore**.
4. You see "(product name) restored and published." The product is back in the shop.

You can also restore from the product's own page. An archived product's page shows the notice "This product is removed from the shop (archived)." with a **Restore** button. Under **Publishing** it says "Status: Archived" instead of the Draft and Published choice, and there is no **Remove from shop** button.

If the product cannot be published, nothing changes and you see "(product name) cannot be published yet." with a list of reasons:
- "It has no price."
- "It has no picture."
- "It has no category."

Then choose one of three buttons:
- **Restore as draft instead**: the product comes back as a draft. You see "(product name) restored as a draft. It is not visible to customers until you publish it."
- **Edit product**: opens the product's page so you can add what is missing. Then press **Restore** again.
- **Dismiss**: closes the message and leaves the product archived.

> Good to know: pressing **Save** on an archived product saves your changes but keeps it archived. Only **Restore** brings it back.
> Good to know: a product restored as a draft is not visible to customers. Publish it later, see "Publishing rules" above.
> Good to know: the first products in the shop may be sample products. Their SKU starts with SEED-. Search for SEED to find them. Remove or replace them with your real products before the shop opens to the public.

## stock | Stock and the products list | roles: owner, support | pages: A6 | group: Products and stock
What changes stock by itself, how to add a delivery, record a shop sale, count your stock, and read the history.
Each product has a stock number. The shop and the website share the same stock. Customers never see the number. They only see "Available" or "Sold out", and they can order at most 10 of one product in one order.

### The products list
Open **Products** in the menu. For each product you see the photo, name, SKU, category (shown by its name, for example Body care), price, stock, status and when and by whom it was last updated. A product with no price says **No price** in bold.

The tabs are:
- **All**: every product that is not removed.
- **Out of stock (n)**: published products with 0 left. They show the **Sold out** badge.
- **Low stock (n)**: published products with 5 or fewer left. They show the **Low stock** badge.
- **Archived**: products you removed. Each row has **Edit** and **Restore** (see below).

Use **Search by name or SKU** to find one product.

### Bringing a removed product back
1. Press the **Archived** tab.
2. Find the product and press **Restore**.
3. You see "(product name) restored and published." The product is back in the shop, with the same stock as before.

> Good to know: a product needs a price, a picture and a category to be published. If one is missing you see "(product name) cannot be published yet." with the reasons. Press **Restore as draft instead** to bring it back hidden from customers, or **Edit product** to add what is missing. More in [Adding and changing products](help:products).

### What changes stock by itself
- A customer places an order: stock goes down.
- You cancel an order that was New or Confirmed: the stock comes back.
- You type **Opening stock** on a new product: stock starts at that number.

### What does not change stock
- Saving the product form never changes stock.
- Restoring a removed product never changes stock.
- A bulk import never changes the stock of a product that already exists. It only sets opening stock for brand new products.
- Cancelling an order that is already Out for delivery does not return stock. Do it yourself (see below).

### Adding a delivery from your supplier
1. In **Products**, press **Adjust stock** next to the product. You can also do this on the product's page, in the **Stock** section.
2. A box called **Adjust stock** opens. It shows the **Current count**.
3. Under **What do you want to do?**, choose **Add stock**.
4. In **Quantity**, type how many arrived.
5. In **Reason**, choose **Restock**.
6. Add a **Note** if you want, for example "Supplier delivery 24 Oct".
7. Press **Confirm**. You see "Stock updated. Now (number)."

### Recording a sale in the physical shop
Walk-in sales also come from the same stock, so record each one (or the day's total for each product).
1. Press **Adjust stock** next to the product.
2. Choose **Remove stock**.
3. In **Quantity**, type how many you sold.
4. In **Reason**, choose **Sold in the shop**.
5. Press **Confirm**.

### Goods that come back or are lost
- Goods returned to the shop, for example after a customer refused a delivery: choose **Add stock** and the reason **Returned goods**.
- Goods that are damaged or lost: choose **Remove stock** and the reason **Damaged or lost**.
- Anything else: use the reason **Other** and write what happened in **Note**.

### Counting your stock (stocktake)
1. Count the product on the shelf.
2. Press **Adjust stock** next to the product.
3. Choose **Set to counted number**.
4. In **Counted number**, type what you counted. The box works out the difference for you.
5. In **Reason**, choose **Stocktake**.
6. Press **Confirm**.

If a customer ordered the product while you were counting, you see "The stock changed while you were working. The latest count has been loaded. Please check it and confirm again." Count again if needed, then confirm. This stops your count from wiping out a new order.

If the number you typed is the same as the **Current count**, nothing is saved. When you press **Confirm**, the box says "The count already matches." under the number. Type a different number and press **Confirm** again.

> Good to know: stock can never go below 0. If you try to remove more than there is, the box refuses.
> Good to know: every change is saved in the history with who made it and when. A change cannot be deleted. To fix a mistake, make a new adjustment that puts it right.
> Good to know: after you change stock, the shop can take up to about a minute and a half to show Available or Sold out.

### The stock history
1. Open the product (press its name in **Products**).
2. In the **Stock** section, look under **Recent movements**. The newest line is at the top.
3. The table has six columns: **When**, **What happened**, **Change**, **Stock after**, **Reason** and **Who**.
4. **When** is in Kampala time.
5. **Change** shows how much the stock went up or down, for example +5 or −2. **Stock after** is the count after that change.
6. **Reason** is the reason chosen when stock was adjusted: Restock, Stocktake, Sold in the shop, Damaged or lost, Returned goods or Other. It is empty for changes made by an order.
7. **Who** is the name of the staff member. For a customer order it says "Customer order".

**What happened** says which of these it was:
- **Opening stock**: the number typed when the product was added.
- **Adjusted by staff**: a change made with **Adjust stock**.
- **Customer order**: a customer ordered the product. The order number is next to it as a link. Press it to open **Orders** already searched for that order.
- **Order cancelled, stock returned**: an order was cancelled before dispatch and the items went back into stock.

For adding or changing the product itself, see [Adding and changing products](help:products).

## bulk-import | Importing many products from a spreadsheet | roles: owner, support | pages: A9 | group: Bulk import
Fill in a spreadsheet, check it, import it, add pictures named after the SKU, and publish the finished drafts.
Use **Import** when you have many products to add or update. It has three steps on one page, called **Import products**. You can stop and come back later. Imported products wait as drafts until you publish them.

### Before you start
- You need a spreadsheet program such as Excel or Google Sheets.
- Each product needs its own SKU, a short code such as ZT-SKIN-001. A picture is matched to a product by the SKU at the start of its file name.
- Plan the pictures: JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB each, up to 6 pictures per product.

### Step 1: the spreadsheet
1. Open **Import** in the menu.
2. Under **Step 1: the spreadsheet**, press **Download the template**. A file called zandy-touch-products.csv is saved. It has the column headings and two example rows.
3. Open it in Excel or Google Sheets.
4. Keep the first line as it is. Replace the example rows with your products, one row per product.
5. Save as CSV UTF-8. In Excel: File, Save As, then choose **CSV UTF-8 (Comma delimited)**. In Google Sheets: File, Download, then **Comma Separated Values (.csv)**.
6. Back on the page, press **Your CSV file** and choose the file. The page shows the file name and size.
7. Press **Check the file**. Nothing is changed yet. The result is called "Check result: nothing has been changed yet".
8. Read the summary, for example "3 rows: 1 to create, 1 to update, 1 rejected". Each row says **Would be created**, **Would be updated** or **Rejected**, and a rejected row says why.
9. When you are happy, press **Import the file**. This button only works after a check that found at least one row to create or update.
10. You see "Import finished. Add the pictures in step 2, then publish in step 3." The result list now says **Created**, **Updated** or **Rejected**.

What goes in each column (press **Columns in the file** on the page to see them):
- sku: required, the product's own code.
- name, brand, category: needed for a new product. For category type the name, for example Body care.
- price: whole shillings, for example 85000. Leave empty for a draft.
- ownBrand and featured: type yes or no.
- shortDescription, description, size, howToUse, ingredients: optional text.
- openingStock: how many you have, used only for new products.

A file can have up to 500 rows and 1 MB.

> Good to know: a row whose SKU already exists updates that product. An empty cell leaves that detail as it is. The product keeps its status, so a published product stays published.
> Good to know: an import never changes the stock of a product that already exists. If you fill in openingStock for such a product, it is ignored and the row says "Ignored because this product already exists. Change stock with Adjust stock."
> Good to know: a rejected row changes nothing. The other good rows still go in.
> Good to know: if the whole file has a problem, such as the wrong format, the page says "The file could not be imported. Nothing was changed." and lists the reasons. Fix the file and check it again.

### Fixing rejected rows
1. Read the reason on the rejected row. It names the column, for example "Price must be a whole number of shillings."
2. Correct those rows in your spreadsheet. You can fix them in the same file.
3. Save as CSV UTF-8 again and choose the file again.
4. Press **Check the file**, then **Import the file**. Rows that were already imported simply update again with the same details.

Other common reasons: the same SKU appears twice in the file (the second row is rejected), the category name does not match one of the six categories, or a new product is missing its name, brand or category.

### Step 2: the pictures
1. Name each picture after its product's SKU. To set the order, add a dash and a number from 1 to 6. For example ZT-SKIN-001-1.jpg is the first picture and ZT-SKIN-001-2.jpg is the second.
2. Under **Step 2: the pictures**, press **Choose the product pictures** and select all the files at once. You can also drop them on the box.
3. The page says how many pictures are ready. Press **Upload (number) pictures**.
4. A progress line shows "Uploading batch 1 of 3". Pictures are sent 20 at a time. Wait until it finishes.
5. You see "Upload finished: (number) attached, (number) not attached." Under **Results per picture**, each file says **Attached** or **Not attached**, with the reason.
6. If some files failed because of the connection, press the button "Try again for (number) pictures that did not upload".
7. Press **Clear results** when you are done reading.

> Good to know: a file whose SKU matches no product is refused and not kept. The reason says "No product has a SKU at the start of this file name." Check the spelling of the file name, or import the product first.
> Good to know: a picture with a position number replaces the picture already at that position. A picture without a number is added at the end.
> Good to know: a product can have only 6 pictures. Further pictures are refused with "This product already has 6 pictures."
> Good to know: pictures bigger than 5 MB or in another format (like PDF) are refused. The reason is shown.

### Step 3: review and publish
1. Under **Step 3: review and publish**, look at the list of drafts. The table has the columns **Picture**, **Name**, **SKU**, **Category**, **Still needs** and **Fix**. The list shows the products from your import. Press **Show all drafts** to see every draft, and **Show only this import** to go back.
2. The **Category** column shows the category name, for example Body care, or "None" if the product has no category yet.
3. The column **Still needs** says **Ready to publish**, or lists what is missing: **Price**, **Picture** or **Category**.
4. For a product that is missing something, press **Edit product** in the **Fix** column and fix it on its own page. See [Adding and changing products](help:products).
5. Press **Publish all complete drafts (number)**. The number is how many are ready.
6. The page says how many products were published, for example "3 products published." Only the complete drafts are published. Products that were not ready are left alone: they stay in the table with their **Still needs** entries, and you can fix them with **Edit product** and publish them later. Only if the shop refuses to publish a product does the message turn yellow and add a list under "These stay drafts:" with the reason for each one.
7. Press **Go to products** to see them in the main list.

> Good to know: drafts are invisible to customers until they are published.
> Good to know: the shop can take up to about a minute and a half to show newly published products.

## messages | Messages and subscribers | roles: owner, support | pages: A10 | group: Messages
Read messages from the contact form and see or download the newsletter list.
Open **Inbox** in the menu. The page is called **Messages and subscribers**. It has two tabs.

### Reading contact messages
1. Open **Inbox** and stay on the **Messages** tab.
2. The newest message is at the top. Each message shows the name, the date and time (in Kampala time), the phone number or email the person gave, and the message.
3. To answer, call, WhatsApp or email the person yourself, using the details shown. There is no reply button.

If there are none, the page says "No messages yet". Messages are also emailed to the shop's owner address.

> Good to know: messages cannot be deleted or marked as read. They are removed automatically about 6 months after they arrive.
> Good to know: the list shows the most recent messages. If you cannot find an older one, ask the owner: every contact message is also sent to the owner's email.

### The subscriber list
These are people who asked in the shop to hear about new products.
1. Open **Inbox** and press the **Subscribers** tab.
2. The table shows **Contact** (an email or a phone number), **Source** (where they signed up), **Subscribed** (the date) and **Active**.
3. **Active** says "No" for people who have unsubscribed. Do not contact them.

### Downloading the list
1. On the **Subscribers** tab, press **Export CSV**.
2. A file called subscribers.csv is downloaded. Open it in Excel or Google Sheets.
3. Use it to send your newsletter or announcements yourself, for example by email or WhatsApp.

> Good to know: the file contains only people who are still subscribed. The screen may show only the first part of a long list, but the download has everyone who is still subscribed.
> Good to know: the website does not send newsletters. You send them yourself from the downloaded list.
> Good to know: only contact people who are on the list, and stop if they ask you to.

## password | Changing your password and forgotten passwords | roles: owner, support | pages: A2, A3, A11 | group: Staff and account
Change your password, and what to do if you forget it.
A password must have at least 10 characters. Very common passwords, like "1234567890", are refused. Use a few words or numbers that only you know.

### Changing your password
1. Open **My account** in the menu.
2. At the top you see your name, email and role.
3. Under **Change password**, type your present password in **Current password**.
4. Type your new password in **New password**.
5. Press **Change password**. You see "Your password has been changed."

If you see "Your current password is not correct.", check the current password and try again. If you see "Use at least 10 characters.", make the new password longer.

> Good to know: when you change your password, you are signed out on your other phones and computers. Sign in there again with the new password.

### If you forgot your password: support staff
Support staff cannot reset their own password. Ask the shop owner for a new temporary password. The owner gives it to you in person or by WhatsApp. Then:
1. Open the **Staff sign in** page and sign in with your email and the temporary password.
2. **My account** opens. Choose your own new password, as described in [Getting started](help:getting-started).

### If you forgot your password: the owner
The reset link goes to the owner's email address only.
1. On the **Staff sign in** page, press **Forgot password?**.
2. On the **Forgot password** page, type your email in **Email**.
3. Press **Send reset link**. The page says "If that address belongs to the owner account, a reset link has been sent to it."
4. Open the owner's email inbox. Look for the message from the shop. Check the spam folder if it is not there.
5. Press the link in the email. The page **Choose a new password** opens.
6. Type a new password in **New password**. It needs at least 10 characters.
7. Press **Change password**. You see "Your password has been changed."
8. Press **Sign in** and sign in with the new password.

> Good to know: the link works only once and only for 60 minutes. If the page says "This link is no longer valid. Ask for a new one.", start again from step 1.
> Good to know: if you type the wrong password 5 times in 15 minutes, signing in is blocked for a short while. Wait, then try again.
> Good to know: if someone else types an email on the forgot-password page, nothing happens unless it is the owner's email, and the page looks the same either way.

## staff-accounts | Staff accounts | roles: owner | pages: A12 | group: Staff and account
Add a support employee and pass on their temporary password, reset a password, or deactivate an account.
Only the owner sees this topic and the **Staff** item in the menu. Support staff can do everything else except this.

### Adding a support employee
1. Open **Staff** in the menu. The page is called **Staff accounts**. It lists everyone with their name, email, role, whether they are active and when they last signed in (in Kampala time, or "Never").
2. Under **Add a support employee**, type the person's **Name** and **Email**.
3. Decide on the box **Email me new-order alerts**. Tick it if this person should receive an email for each new order. Leave it empty if not. (The word "me" is misleading here: it applies to the new employee.)
4. Press **Add employee**.
5. A yellow box appears: "Temporary password for (name). It is shown only once. Pass it on in person or by WhatsApp; it is never emailed." The password is shown under it.
6. Write it down or copy it, and give it to the employee in person or by WhatsApp.
7. Press **I have noted it** only after you have saved it. The password disappears.

The employee then signs in and chooses their own password. See [Getting started](help:getting-started).

> Good to know: the temporary password is shown only once. If you lose it, use **Reset password** to make a new one.
> Good to know: if the page says "That email is already in use.", that email already has an account.
> Good to know: a support employee can do everything except manage staff accounts. They cannot see this page.

### Resetting a staff password
Use this when an employee forgets their password, or when you lose the temporary password.
1. Open **Staff**.
2. Find the person and press **Reset password** in their row.
3. A box asks "Reset password for (name)?" It says a new temporary password is issued and their sessions end. Press **Reset password**.
4. The yellow box shows the new temporary password, once. Pass it on, then press **I have noted it**.

The old password stops working at once and the person is signed out everywhere.

### Deactivating an account
Use this when someone leaves.
1. Open **Staff**.
2. Press **Deactivate** in the person's row.
3. A box says "Their sessions end immediately. Their name stays on past history." Press **Deactivate**.
4. You see "Account deactivated." The row now says No under **Active**.

> Good to know: deactivating cannot be undone in the staff area. The person can never sign in again with that account.
> Good to know: the person's name stays on the orders and stock changes they made.
> Good to know: your own row, the owner's, has no **Reset password** or **Deactivate** button. To change your own password, see [Changing your password](help:password).
