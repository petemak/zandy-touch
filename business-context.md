# Business Context

## What we do
[Zandy Touch is a beauty and wellness business based in Kampala, Uganda,  specializing in skincare products, perfumes, body-shaping products, body mists, sprays, and personal care solutions. We are committed to providing quality products that help our customers feel confident, beautiful, and satisfied. We currently reach customers through social media, referrals, and personal connections. We also have a physical shop in Kampala where walk-in clients can choose and buy products.  Our goal is to establish a professional online shop where customers can conveniently browse our product range, view product details and prices, and place orders from anywhere.

The online shop will provide a smooth and enjoyable shopping experience, making it easy for customers to discover products, order conveniently, and receive their purchases through reliable delivery services. We aim to expand our reach across Uganda and out side the country to reach more potential customers.]

## Customers 
[Target customers are local customers in their early 20s to late 30s.  These customers look for stylish and quality products beyond those found on the local market. They are urban professionals who value style and are willing to spend on quality. They find products online, social media and word of mouth. Once trust is established they view the products online and order, knowing that the products will satisfy their needs based on previious experience ]
