# Business Context

## What we do
[Zandy Touch is a beauty and wellness business based in Kampala, Uganda,  specializing in the following product groups:

 1. Skincare
 2. Perfumes
 3. Body-shaping products
 4. Body care
 5. Weight loss 
 6. Health & Wellness 

Dietary supplements and the weight-gain porridge belong to Health & Wellness. There is no "Bedroom" category.

We are committed to providing quality products that help our customers feel confident, beautiful, and satisfied. We currently reach customers through social media, referrals, and personal connections. We also have a physical shop in Kampala where walk-in clients can choose and buy products.  Our goal is to establish a professional online shop where customers can conveniently browse our product range, view product details and prices, and place orders from anywhere.

The online shop will provide a smooth and enjoyable shopping experience, making it easy for customers to discover products, order conveniently, and receive their purchases through reliable delivery services. We aim to expand our reach across Uganda and outside the country to reach more potential customers.

The webshop should provide the following functionality for three roles: shopowner, customer and support employees.

For the shop owner (root account):
- sign in/out
- Manage the product catalogue
- Add/remove support employee account. 
- View orders
- Process orders and approve delivery
- Add products (description, price, images) to the product catalogue
- Update product details (description, price, images)
- Remove products from the product catalogue
- Bulk import products: names, descriptions, prices and the related images in one go (a spreadsheet plus photos)


For appointed support personnel:
- sign in/out
- View orders
- Process orders and approve delivery
- Add products (description, price, images) to the product catalogue
- Update product details (description, price, images)
- Remove products from the product catalogue
- Bulk import products: names, descriptions, prices and the related images in one go (a spreadsheet plus photos)

Customer:
- Browse product catalogue
- Show product detailed description
- Add product to shopping cart
- View shopping cart
- Enter name, address and submit order

Customers access webshop and order as guests. Payment in cash or mobile money on delivery. Delivery costs are negotiated with the customer.

Further rules:
- A product must have a price before it can be made available for ordering online.
- Stock is counted: each product has a stock quantity, ordering reduces it, and a product with no stock shows as sold out.
- Staff cannot enter orders on behalf of customers. All orders are placed by customers through the webshop.
- Policy texts (terms of sale, privacy, returns, order terms) are drafted as placeholders and reviewed by a human before launch.]

Delivery
- Delivery cities: Kampala, Entebbe, Jinja, Mbale, Mbarara and Fort Portal (regional cities in the south, east and west of Uganda).
- Delivery cost and timing are agreed with the customer when the order is confirmed.

Stock and orders
- The physical shop and the webshop share one stock pool. Staff record walk-in sales as stock adjustments.
- "Low stock" is 5 or fewer; a customer can order at most 10 of one product per order.
- An order cancelled after dispatch does not restore stock automatically; staff adjust stock by hand when goods come back.
- Orders are not edited: to change one, staff cancel it and the customer orders again.
- Customer email is optional and used only for an order receipt.

Policies and data
- Policy texts are reviewed by a human: the owner is consulted manually before any text is approved.
- Returns and cancellation (owner, 26 October 2026): customers can cancel an order at any time before it is delivered.
  Returns are not accepted once an order has been delivered (shop owner, 26 October 2026; this replaces an earlier
  24-hour unopened-returns rule). Products are delivered in their original packaging; customers check that the packaging is intact and
  that they received what they ordered, without opening the products. Damage in transit is settled between the shop and the
  delivery agent (rare); the policy only asks the customer to report visibly damaged packaging to the delivery person. The four policy texts were reviewed and approved by the owner on 26 October 2026 (version 2026-10-26).
- Customer personal data (contact details on orders, contact messages) is kept for 6 months, then removed.
- Original uploaded photos are deleted after the resized versions are made.
- Product pictures are not a feature of their own: a picture always belongs to a product. There is no separate photo
  library. Pictures are added on the product itself or come in with a bulk import.
- Bulk import: the owner fills a CSV spreadsheet (one row per product, saved from Excel or Google Sheets) and uploads it
  together with the product photos, each photo named after the product's SKU (for example ZT-SKIN-001-1.jpg). Imported
  products are drafts until staff review and publish them. A row whose SKU already exists updates that product.
  An import never changes stock of an existing product; stock changes go through stock adjustments.
- Staff help: the admin has built-in help with step-by-step instructions for every admin task (orders, products,
  stock, bulk import, staff accounts, messages, passwords). It is written and kept up to date by the agent team,
  not by the owner. Each person sees only the topics for their role: support staff do not see owner-only tasks
  such as managing staff accounts. Screenshots are added later, once the screens are final.
- Catalogue pages may be served from cache for up to a day if the backend is down; logo and icons are cached for 1 day.]
